N° 01 Our Story

Brewed slowly. Shared warmly.

We started with one simple belief — that a great cup of coffee can turn an ordinary day into a beautiful one.

Every bean we pour is ethically sourced and roasted in small batches. Every plate is made from scratch the morning you order it. And every corner of our space was designed for lingering — for first dates, long catch-ups, quiet laptops and loud laughter.

— The FoundersAurelia Café
