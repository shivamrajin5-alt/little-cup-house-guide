N° 01 Our Story

Two years. Countless full tables.

We opened on Danapur–Khagaul Road in August 2024 with one simple belief — nothing brings people together like good food.

Two years of delicious memories, laughter and unforgettable moments later, that belief still runs our kitchen. Kebabs come straight off the tandoor, noodles straight out of the wok, and every curry is cooked the way you'd want it at home — at prices that feel fair to every family, student and group of friends who walks in.

— Team New Hungry HopeMustafapur, Patna

Tandoor & Grill

Tandoori chicken, tikka, malai and kali mirch kebabs, charred in the clay oven.

Wok, Momos & Rolls
